Research

My current work studies where and how multimodal evidence should influence recommendation. I am particularly interested in structured routing, coarse-to-fine prediction, and decoding procedures that can revise an uncertain decision rather than commit too early.

A longer-term thread asks how an intelligent system can retain experience, retrieve it for the right question, and improve without reducing memory to a passive store of information.

Beyond research

I have also built and operated small e-commerce stores across Taobao, 1688, and Pinduoduo. Working from product research and supplier coordination through listings, promotion, customer service, and inventory taught me to test ideas against real constraints. One new store reached more than RMB 100,000 in cumulative sales, while a seasonal product experiment reached RMB 30,000 in its first 20 days.

I treat this experience as part of how I think: be willing to take a risk, stay close to users, and measure what actually happened.
