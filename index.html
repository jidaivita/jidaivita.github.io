About

I am currently a master's student at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications. I earned my bachelor's degree in Internet of Things Engineering from BUPT in 2024.

My research focuses on recommender systems, multimodal learning, and generative modeling. I am particularly interested in how models route evidence, revise uncertain decisions, and preserve useful experience over time.

I am preparing for further research opportunities. If our interests overlap, please feel free to email me.
