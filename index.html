About

I am currently a master's student at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, advised by Prof. Quan Fang and Prof. Jun Hu. I earned my bachelor's degree in Internet of Things Engineering from BUPT in 2024.

My research interests span data mining, embodied intelligence, and general physical intelligence. I am interested in how learning systems discover structure in data, learn through interaction, and retain useful physical knowledge across tasks.

I am preparing for further research opportunities. If our interests overlap, please feel free to email me.
